Help

Answers to the questions players ask most. Settings are in the game’s Menu (the button at the top right) → Settings.

Where is my progress saved?

Your collection, coins and settings are saved on this device. Deleting the game also removes its local progress. Game Center records achievements and scores separately.

How do I turn off sounds or music?

Menu → Settings → Sound or Music. Vibration can be switched off there too.

How do I start over?

Menu → Settings → Start over, then confirm with Yes, start from scratch. This deletes your collection, coins and items and cannot be undone.

Do I need an internet connection?

Puzzles and the gallery work offline. Game Center and links to museums need an internet connection.

Contact us

For gameplay questions or a problem with a painting, open a public support request. Include your game version and describe what happened. Please leave out personal information.
