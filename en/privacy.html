Privacy Policy

25 November 2026

Saved on your device

Your collection, purchases made with in-game coins, settings and progress are stored on your device. You can reset progress in the game settings. The game has no advertising and you can play without creating an account.

TelemetryDeck

In version 1.0, TelemetryDeck analytics are disabled. The game does not send gameplay statistics to TelemetryDeck.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, the game submits your earned achievements and collection score to Apple. You can manage Game Center in your device settings.

Notifications and external links

Reminders are scheduled on your device with your permission. You can turn them off in iOS Settings. Opening an external artwork or museum resource connects your device to its provider, whose privacy policy applies.
